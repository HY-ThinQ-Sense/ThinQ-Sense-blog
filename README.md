## Research Idea

### Title

**ThinQ Sense: Privacy-Preserving Multimodal Perception for Proactive AI Home**

### Proposal

Smart homes can monitor connected appliances but often lack real-time awareness of who is present, where people are, and what they are doing. We propose ThinQ Sense, a privacy-preserving multimodal perception system that combines camera-based person and posture recognition with optional mmWave-based presence and position sensing. Lightweight models process sensor data locally and output structured contextual events instead of transmitting raw audio or video to a home agent. A context engine aggregates these events into a household state model, enabling proactive appliance decisions. Our prototype will investigate recognition accuracy, inference latency, and sensor fusion under different conditions. We will demonstrate adaptive robot vacuum scheduling that avoids occupied rooms, as well as predictive bedtime preparation, using live sensing and a digital twin simulator. The project aims to show how local AI perception can improve smart home automation while limiting exposure of sensitive data.
